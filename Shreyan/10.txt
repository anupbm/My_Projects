The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) have appointed Shane Deitz as the head coach of the Pakistan women’s cricket team.

Deitz will take charge ahead of Pakistan’s upcoming bilateral series against West Indies and will join the squad in the Caribbean for his first assignment with the team.

The 51-year-old brings extensive experience to the role, having most recently served as head coach of the West Indies women’s team.

During his tenure, the Caribbean side reached the semi-finals in consecutive editions of the ICC Women’s T20 World Cup (2024 and 2026).

Deitz has also previously worked with the Bangladesh and Netherlands women’s national teams. Between 2008 and 2013, he served as a high-performance coach with Cricket Wellington in New Zealand.
